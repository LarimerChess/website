Third Saturday Slow

A US Chess rated tournament of the Larimer County Chess Club in Fort Collins, Colorado. Next: Saturday, November 21, 2026, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Details

Cost: $15 entry, free for players under 18. If the fee is a hardship, pay what you can; no US Chess member is turned away.
Time control: G/70;d10 (70 minutes per player, with a 10-second delay on each move). Regular rated.

A monthly US Chess rated tournament with a long time control, for players who want time to think. In Old Town Fort Collins.

Format: 3-round Swiss.

Round times

  • Round 1: 10:00 AM
  • Round 2: 1:30 PM, after a lunch break
  • Round 3: 4:30 PM

If a round runs long, the next one starts as soon as it finishes.

A current US Chess membership is required. Join or renew at new.uschess.org/join-us-chess (opens in a new tab)

Players of every strength welcome. Bring a board and clock if you have one. Clocks must support delay. Coffee and water are provided.

Players under 18 may come on their own. Parents remain responsible for them; the club doesn't supervise minors.

Meet on the main floor at Peak Community Church in Old Town Fort Collins.

Questions: president@larimerchess.org

Register

This is an invitational: registration is closed, and the tournament director registers the players. The entries below are public.

Online registration isn't open yet. To register, email president@larimerchess.org.
