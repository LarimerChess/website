Knightmare Arena Classical

A US Chess rated tournament of the Larimer County Chess Club in Fort Collins, Colorado.

Dates

  • Saturday, October 31, 2026, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

Details

Cost: $15 entry, free for players under 18. If the fee is a hardship, pay what you can; no US Chess member is turned away.
Time control: 30 minutes + 30 seconds per move.

A Halloween arena tournament at classical speed. Play as many games as you can between 10:00 AM and 5:30 PM. The faster you finish, the more games you get.

How it works

  • When your game ends, report your result and you'll be paired with the next free player.
  • Win = 2 points, draw = 1, loss = 0.
  • Possessed: after two wins in a row, each further win is worth 3 points until you lose or draw.
  • Blood Pact: before a game, halve your clock and increment to 15 minutes + 15 seconds per move. Win and you get a bonus point, as long as your opponent kept the full 30 + 30.
  • You won't face the same opponent twice in a row.
  • No new games start after 5:30 PM. Games in progress finish and count.
  • Most points wins.

Come for the whole day or drop in for a few games. Take a break whenever you like; just stay out of the queue until you're back.

A current US Chess membership is required. Join or renew at new.uschess.org/join-us-chess (opens in a new tab)

US Chess rated. Players of every strength welcome. Bring a board and clock if you have one.

Players under 18 may come on their own. Parents remain responsible for them; the club doesn't supervise minors.

Meet on the main floor at Peak Community Church in Old Town Fort Collins.

Questions: president@larimerchess.org
