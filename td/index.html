      Incidents

      Warnings, penalties, game losses, expulsions, and bars, under the Safe Play policy, §8. Log one from a player's Incident button. Expelled and removed players drop off that date's entries, so they aren't paired again; barred players can't register. Those three email the President.

        Log an incident

        Only the President may bar a player (§8). The Incidents tab of the registration Sheet keeps the log; reasons never appear on the website.
