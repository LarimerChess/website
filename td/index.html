Run the tournament

Starting adds everyone registered; later entrants can be added before any round. Swiss: US Chess rules 27 to 29. Quads: round robins of four, 30G. Arena: players wait in a queue and are paired as they finish games, until the cutoff.
For a club night's month, one round a night.
Sections Each section is paired and ranked on its own. A player goes in the section with the lowest limit their rating is under, unrated players too, and can be moved up before round 1. Leave the limit empty for an open section.

Gets a reminder each morning, from the second morning after play ends, until US Chess has rated the tournament.

This round

Everyone starts as Present, or as a half-point bye if they asked for one this round; change anyone who isn't here, or who asked and plays after all. Anyone else's round is unplayed, unless they asked for a half-point bye (22C) or a zero-point bye. A round can be reclassified later, under Standings.

      Add an extra games section

      Rated games for players who are also in another section, such as everyone playing again once the tournament is over (28M4). They are rated with the tournament, as a section of their own in its rating report, and don't count in the other sections' standings. The section starts with the next round to pair, alongside any rounds the other sections have left.

      Rating report

      Upload these three files in MUIR, US Chess's ratings portal, under New Event, or enter the results there by hand, and review the event before submitting. The tournament is complete once US Chess shows it rated with these results; until then, from the second morning after play ends, the chief TD and the President get a reminder each morning.

      As US Chess writes it, from the event's calendar description when it's still listed.

      The desk can keep the files in a folder on this computer: it rewrites them whenever the tournament loads with every result in, and deletes them when a round or result is missing, or the tournament is restarted, so a file never holds results the desk no longer has.

      Or download them, which reloads the tournament first. A downloaded file stays as it was: after any change, delete it and download again.

      Register a player

        US Chess ID, or first or last name. Players who have registered before, then the club's contacts, come up as you type; an eight-digit ID not among them, or a contact picked from the list, is looked up at US Chess.

        Byes

        Rounds the player will miss, each a half-point bye.

        With an email, the player gets the confirmation and a withdraw link. For a player under 18, a parent's or guardian's.

          Standings and byes

          Standings

          Tiebreaks in the US Chess default order (34E): modified median, Solkoff, cumulative, cumulative of opposition.

          Byes and unplayed rounds

          Every round a player had no game in. Change one if it was classified wrongly, such as an unplayed round the TD excuses as a half-point bye; the score follows.

          Money

          What the Payments tab records for this choice, a month with its nights. Paying by card adds the card fee; marking a player unpaid adds a correction instead of erasing the payment.

            Incidents

            Warnings, penalties, game losses, expulsions, and bars, under the Safe Play policy, §8. Log one from a player's Incident button. Expelled and removed players drop off that date's entries, so they aren't paired again; barred players can't register. Those three email the President.

              Contacts

              Contacts
              The registration Sheet's Contacts tab, the club's only list of everyone it knows. Changes here save to the Sheet, which keeps its version history; edits made in the Sheet show here after the desk reloads. A contact's US Chess ID lets the player search find them by it.
