    About us

    The Larimer County Chess Club is a new club for chess players in Larimer County, Colorado. We are forming as a Colorado nonprofit and will apply for 501(c)(3) status. What we do:

    • Regular community play every week
    • Tournaments rated by US Chess
    • Chess instruction for youth, students and adults
    • No event fees for youth players
    • Club dues, if any, are still to be decided

    Everyone is welcome. You don't need to belong to any organization to play, including the organizations whose buildings we use. To play in US Chess rated games and tournaments, you do need a US Chess membership.
