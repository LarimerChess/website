Larimer County Chess Club

A new Fort Collins chess club for players of every age and strength. Weekly club nights and US Chess rated tournaments.

Get involved

Club night

Mondays from 6:30 PM until the last game ends, starting October 12, 2026, at Peak Community Church in Old Town Fort Collins. Just show up. Club games are US Chess events, so you need a current US Chess membership.

Peak Community Church
500 Mathews St.
Fort Collins, CO 80524

Saturday tournaments

Monthly US Chess rated tournaments upstairs at Peak Community Church in Fort Collins.

  • First Saturdays from November 7, 2026, and fourth Saturdays from January 23, 2027: Classic. 4-round Swiss at G/30;+30, dual rated. Rounds at 10:00 AM, 12:15, 2:30 and 4:45 PM.
  • Third Saturdays from November 21, 2026: Slow. 3-round Swiss at G/70;d10, regular rated. Rounds at 10:00 AM, 1:30 and 4:30 PM.
  • Saturday, October 31, 2026: Knightmare Arena Classical. A Halloween arena at 30+30 from 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Play as many games as you can; the faster you finish, the more you play. Unrated.

Entry is $15, free for players under 18. If the fee is a hardship, pay what you can; no US Chess member is turned away. Every event needs a current US Chess membership. Players under 18 need a parent or another designated adult on site.

    About us

    The Larimer County Chess Club is a new club for chess players in Fort Collins and across Larimer County, Colorado. We are forming as a Colorado nonprofit and will apply for 501(c)(3) status. What we do:

    • Regular community play every week
    • Tournaments rated by US Chess
    • No event fees for youth players
    • Club dues, if any, are still to be decided

    Everyone is welcome. You don't need to belong to the organizations whose buildings we use. All club events are US Chess events, so you do need a current US Chess membership to play.
